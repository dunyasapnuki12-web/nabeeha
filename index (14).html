About me

Creative thinking, organised execution.

I’m Nabeeha, a social media and digital marketing professional interested in creating useful, engaging content that supports a brand’s goals. I enjoy planning content calendars, developing ideas for Reels and posts, writing captions, and reviewing performance indicators to identify opportunities for improvement.

My workflow can include AI tools for brainstorming and drafting, alongside human review for brand voice, accuracy, and quality. I aim to keep content consistent, audience-focused, and aligned with each brand’s identity.

My approach

Plan, publish, learn, improve.

A repeatable process helps keep content consistent while leaving room to learn from the audience.

Understand

Clarify the audience, offer, brand voice, goals, and competitors.

Plan

Build content pillars, a calendar, formats, hooks, and calls to action.

Create

Produce and review content for accuracy, clarity, quality, and consistency.

Measure

Review reach, watch time, saves, shares, clicks, and conversions when data is available.
